About TimeGraphBot ↗

A project in development

Structured memory
for public data.

Public information changes. TimeGraph is being built to preserve a structured, verifiable record of what was published, when it was observed, how it changed, and the evidence behind each observation.

Learn about TimeGraphBot ↗

The approach

A clearer history of public information.

01

What was published

Capture the public source and the information it made available.

02

When it was observed

Record the time of each observation so changes have context.

03

How it changed

Connect observations into a history that can be checked against supporting evidence.

First area of focus

Italian municipalities and local public administration.

TimeGraph is starting with publicly accessible information from local institutions in Italy. The project is in development; this site explains its intent and the planned behavior of its crawler.
